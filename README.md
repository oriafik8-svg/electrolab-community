# ElectroLab Community

Free 3D models and electronic circuits made with [ElectroLab](https://github.com/oriafik8-svg/electrolab) and shared by its users.

- **Browse and use:** open ElectroLab, choose **Community** in the side menu. Everything here is also readable on GitHub.
- **Share yours:** in ElectroLab press **Community, then Share…**. The app sends a *pull request* for you. A maintainer looks at it, and once it is approved it shows up for everyone.
- Each item lives in `items/<name>/` with an `item.json` (title, author, license), an optional model (`.stl` / `.3mf`), an optional circuit (`project.json` + `sketch.ino`) and a picture.
- `index.json` is built automatically; do not edit it by hand.

## Licenses
Every item states its own license (CC0, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 or MIT). Read it before you reuse something. By sharing, you confirm that you made the work or have the right to share it under the license you chose.

## Rules
Only share things you made yourself or that are explicitly free to share. No copyrighted designs, trademarks, weapons, or anything unsafe or hateful. Maintainers may remove items; to report one, open an issue.
